## 👋 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer and Automation Developer** focused on designing and building digital solutions that solve real business problems.

My work sits at the intersection of **software engineering, intelligent automation, data, system integration, and business processes**. I enjoy understanding how an operation works, identifying inefficiencies, designing the technical architecture, and turning the process into a reliable and maintainable solution.

Over the last year, my focus has expanded beyond traditional RPA into **full application development, API integration, PostgreSQL, Google Apps Script, AppSheet, data transformation, infrastructure planning, and AI-assisted development**.

I have also been gaining hands-on knowledge of **airport cargo operations**, developing solutions around operational workflows, aircraft attention, cargo reception, reservations, dimensions, billing, data synchronization, and process monitoring.

> **My approach:** understand the business first, design the data and architecture carefully, automate what makes sense, and build software that can actually be used in production.
